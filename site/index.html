How we work

Clear scope, fixed price and a delivery date agreed before work starts.

  1. Brief

    You send plans, elevations or a 3D model, with references and the views you need.

  2. Quote

    We confirm scope, price and delivery date in writing, usually within one working day.

  3. Composition

    We send draft camera views for your approval before rendering.

  4. Materials and lighting

    You comment on near-final images. Two rounds of revisions are included.

  5. Delivery

    Final high-resolution images, unbranded and ready for print and web.
